# Membrane Lab — OCR A Biology: Expanded Real Past-Paper Links

## Start
- Open `Membrane_Lab_Past_Papers_Expanded_Offline.html` in Chrome, Safari, Edge or Firefox. It is a self-contained offline HTML app (lessons, quizzes and infographics work without the internet). External past-paper links require internet.
- Or unzip `Membrane_Lab_Past_Papers_Expanded_Website.zip` and open its `index.html` or upload all files to a static hosting service.
- Visit the **Past Papers** tab and filter by **Topic PDFs**, **Online questions** and **Official OCR papers**. You can also search the year, paper or topic. Every resource opens its exact external file or page in a new tab.

## Paper links (20 entries)
- 3 pre-existing PMT membrane-only QP/MS pairs
- 2 added Study Mind membrane-only Part 1 and Part 2 pairs on Google Drive
- 3 topic-specific external online question pages (A-level Biology Tutor x2, Save My Exams)
- 12 official OCR full-exam QP/MS pairs, including A-level H420/01 and AS H020/01, H020/02, spanning 2017–2024.
- The 2023 H420/01 entry now links directly to its matching 2023 OCR mark scheme.

IMPORTANT: Full OCR past papers contain many biology topics, not just membranes. Topic pages from Save My Exams may contain original exam-style questions, not necessarily reproduced OCR questions. The interactive 30 MCQs and 30 MSQs within the app are original practice questions, not OCR past-paper reproductions. Google Drive documents or third-party sites may require permission, login or subscription. Links may change as publishers update their sites.

## Preserved features
Five lessons, five educational simulations, 30 original MCQs plus 30 MSQs, 30 quick questions, 22 fill-in-the-blanks, flashcards, matching, hotspots, 15 written exam questions, diagrams, data-practical tools, and a glossary. Study progress is stored locally.
